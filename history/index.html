Methodology and provenance

Official result: raw wins decide final standings and titles. The 17-game-equivalent normalization is only a comparison tool for seasons in which teams played different numbers of games.

Best-available regret: this is a one-change-per-pick hindsight measure. It asks how many wins a drafter left on the board at that moment while preserving all earlier actual picks; it is not a replay of the whole draft.

Perfect replay: this separate counterfactual is the globally coherent highest-win remaining selection at each pick, using a stable team ID tiebreak. It describes hindsight, not a strategy available before the season.

Names: participant aliases from source workbooks are merged under canonical display names. This prevents one person from appearing as separate careers when a workbook used a nickname.
